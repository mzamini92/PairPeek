# Pair & Peek Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Pair & Peek is an iPhone game developed by Mohamad Zamini. This policy describes data handling in version 1.0 and the support page.

## Overview

Pair & Peek does not operate a developer-hosted account or gameplay database. The app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information. Optional Apple services, nearby multiplayer, and support requests involve the data described below.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves gameplay progress, settings, current boards, achievements, and related game state locally. When you connect Game Center, a player identifier is stored with local medal progress to keep medals associated with the correct player.

Purchased boost balances, transaction identifiers, and recovery information are stored in the device Keychain to deliver purchases accurately and avoid duplicate credits. The developer does not receive your payment card details. The app does not provide cross-device synchronization of progress or boost balances.

## Apple purchases and Game Center

Apple processes in-app purchases. The app receives verified transaction information, such as product and transaction identifiers, to credit boosts and handle transaction changes.

If you connect Game Center, the app accesses your Game Center player identifier and display name and submits your Perfect Medals total to Apple. Your Game Center profile and score may be visible to other players according to Apple’s services and your settings. These features are subject to [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Nearby multiplayer

When you host or join a nearby game, Apple’s Multipeer Connectivity framework discovers and connects nearby devices. Your chosen nickname and a generated session identifier identify you in the lobby. Connected players exchange board information, readiness, moves, timing, progress, and results to run and rank the race. Your nickname and race results are visible to participants; hosting can make your room discoverable to nearby players.

Connections require encryption. Race information is exchanged between participating devices, not through a developer-operated game server. Local Network access is used for this feature, not to collect GPS location. Leave the lobby or race to stop participating, and manage Local Network permission in iPhone Settings.

## Sharing and support

Using the Share action sends the result text or challenge information you choose to the destination you select.

Support is provided through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/mzamini92/PairPeek/issues/new?title=Privacy%20or%20support%3A%20). If you contact us there, we can see your GitHub username and the information you submit and use it to respond and investigate the issue. Issues are public and require a GitHub account. Do not post passwords, payment details, receipts, or other sensitive information. GitHub hosts these pages and handles website information under its [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Retention and your choices

Local game state remains until you reset it or remove the app’s local data. Reset Progress does not remove purchased boost balances or Apple’s previously submitted Game Center scores. Keychain purchase records may persist after app deletion; uninstalling is not a guaranteed way to erase them. The developer has no remote access to your device’s local data.

You can choose not to use purchases, Game Center, multiplayer, or sharing. Manage Apple service privacy settings through your Apple Account and device settings. Apple controls retention of its purchase and Game Center records. GitHub support discussions remain until removed, subject to GitHub’s policies; contact us about removing information from a support issue. Do not include the sensitive information again in the request.

## Children

The app does not ask players to provide their age or contact details. It has no advertising or third-party analytics. Parents or guardians can manage purchases and Game Center through Apple’s parental controls. Children should not post personal information in public support requests.

## Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s data practices change.
