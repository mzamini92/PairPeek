# Pair & Peek
### Small treasures. Big joy.

A picture-matching game for iPhone with warm golden artwork and beautiful collections to explore.

## A little play, a brighter day

- **120 campaign levels:** Study the preview and find matching pairs before running out of chances. Free retries bring fresh pictures and positions.
- **Endless Journey:** Choose Relaxed, Balanced, or Challenging play and your favorite picture collections.
- **Time Ladder:** Climb from 4×4 to 10×10. Each board begins with 30 seconds. Four consecutive matches earn three seconds; a mistake costs three seconds.
- **Three’s Company:** Find matching sets of three.
- **Nearby challenges:** Host or join a local race with friends.
- **Perfect Medals:** Complete campaign levels without mistakes or assistance. Game Center rankings require sign-in and connectivity.

Explore 189 pictures across animals, rare flowers, planets and space, desserts, Zodiac, Luxury Living, Vintage Treasures, Dream Destinations, and Original Wonders.

**No ads.** Optional purchases offer 10 Extra Peeks or 10 Time Boosts. Each Time Boost adds three seconds. Prices are displayed in the app for your region. Retrying a failed game is free.

**Version 1.0 is being prepared for the App Store.** A download link will be added when available.

### Gameplay help

**Campaign:** The third wrong guess ends a normal attempt. Retry for free with fresh pictures and positions.

**Extra Peeks:** Reveal the board for two seconds. Continuing after a campaign game over also gives one more chance on the same board.

**Time Ladder:** Every board starts with 30 seconds. Four consecutive matches earn three seconds. A mistake removes three seconds and resets the streak. Unused time does not carry over. Odd-sized boards have a free center; clearing 10×10 wins the ladder.

**Time Boosts:** Each adds three seconds, with a maximum of two boosts per board.

**Perfect Medals:** Finish a campaign level without mistakes, Peeks, or assistance. Replaying the same level does not earn another medal for that level.

### Purchase help

Each optional pack contains 10 Extra Peeks or 10 Time Boosts. If a purchase appears missing:

1. Check whether Apple confirmed it rather than leaving it pending or canceled.
2. Reopen the app with an internet connection and check your balance.
3. If it is still incorrect, contact support using the link above. Do not publish your receipt or Apple Account information.

Boost balances are stored on your device and do not sync across devices. Resetting game progress keeps the boost balance. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step.

Apple handles billing and refund requests: [Apple purchase support](https://support.apple.com/billing).

### Nearby friends

Use current, compatible app versions on both iPhones. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and allow Local Network access for Pair & Peek in iPhone Settings. One player hosts and the others join. Stay nearby throughout the race. If nobody appears, check permissions on both phones and reopen the lobby.

### Game Center and saved progress

Sign in to Game Center on your iPhone and connect from the Friends tab. Rankings require an internet connection.

Progress is stored locally. Cross-device progress synchronization is not available. Game Center rankings do not restore your local campaign progress or boost inventory. Resetting local progress does not erase a previously submitted Game Center best score.
